Install

  1. Download the APKTap “Download for Android” above and open the file.
  2. Allow the installIf asked, let your browser or Files app “install unknown apps”. If Play Protect warns you, choose More details → Install anyway.
  3. Turn the duck on in AccessibilityOpen the app, tap “turn me on”, pick Scroll Duck under Accessibility and switch it on.
Can’t switch it on? Android 13+ restricts apps installed outside the Play Store. Go to Settings → Apps → Scroll Duck, tap ⋮ in the top corner → Allow restricted settings, then try again.

FAQ

How do I update?

The app checks when you open it. When there’s a new version you’ll see “a new me is here! 🎁”; tap update me and you’re done, your data stays. You can also download again from this page and install over it.

Does the duck read my screen?

No. It only looks at screen IDs to tell when you’re on Reels or Shorts, never at text or content. Only what you type or say in the chat is sent to the AI so it can reply.

Why does it need Accessibility?

To know which app is open, show the duck over the video, and pause the video when the duck shows up.

I don’t see the 3D duck?

The 3D duck needs Android 13 or newer. On Android 11–12 everything else still works.
